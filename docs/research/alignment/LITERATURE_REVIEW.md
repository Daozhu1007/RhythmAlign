# Alignment literature and adversarial novelty search

Study baseline: `ffa6b07a591dbdb54ae8350f3d27dce3ed74c847`. Search completed 2026-09-12. This is a focused research review, not a systematic review with a claim of exhaustive coverage. It concerns temporal alignment only.

## Search method and evidential limits

Queries covered audio synchronization, fingerprint verification, GCC-PHAT, chroma/onset synchronization, PCEN, correlation-maximum significance, reject options, selective risk, confidence calibration, and multi-feature fusion. Searches explicitly included combinations of *alignment + confidence + rejection*, *fingerprint + GCC + verification*, and *correlation maxima + false detection*. Original proceedings, journal pages, author manuscripts, and official implementation documentation were preferred. Search-engine dates were not used as publication dates.

The primary question was whether heterogeneous audio evidence followed by rejection of unsupported or ambiguous offsets was already known. **Yes.** The closest practical precedent found is Duong, Howson and Legallais (2012). Its fingerprint stage proposes candidate offsets; GCC-PHAT verifies them; confidence thresholds can trigger acquisition of more audio. This is a direct precedent for multi-stage evidence verification with refusal to commit, although its representations, acquisition setting, and thresholds differ from RhythmAlign.

No claim is made that every threshold arrangement in Engine v2 has appeared verbatim. A previously unseen arrangement of existing thresholds is not, by itself, a methodological contribution. Conversely, a useful new benchmark or a reproducible domain-specific failure analysis could become an empirical contribution if independently validated.

## PCEN and HPSS: useful ingredients, limited novelty

**Wang, Y., Getreuer, P., Hughes, T., Lyon, R. F., Saurous, R. A. (2017)**, *Trainable frontend for robust and far-field keyword spotting*, ICASSP, 5670–5674, is a conference paper with a 2016 preprint. PCEN supplies adaptive gain control and dynamic-range compression; using it as an alignment representation is an application choice, not invention of PCEN. [Author publication page](https://getreuer.info/papers/wang2017trainable/index.html), [original paper](https://research.google.com/pubs/archive/45911.pdf).

**Lostanlen et al. (2019)**, *Per-Channel Energy Normalization: Why and How*, IEEE Signal Processing Letters 26(1), 39–43, studies PCEN's statistical behavior. Its results do not imply that a maximum of a summed, temporally differenced, HPSS-processed correlation curve follows a standard normal null. The DOI and journal metadata identify SPL; the author's landing-page venue label should not override that. [Author page](https://www.lostanlen.com/pubs/lostanlen2019spl/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/LSP.2018.2878620).

**FitzGerald, D. (2010)**, *Harmonic/Percussive Separation Using Median Filtering*, DAFx, is a conference paper establishing median-filter HPSS. Here its only relevance is the harmonic representation used before alignment. Engine v2 applies HPSS to a mel-power array; that precise placement is not the original linear-frequency magnitude formulation. [Proceedings PDF](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2010/DAFx10/DerryFitzGerald_DAFx10_P15.pdf).

The **librosa 0.11.0 PCEN documentation** is implementation documentation, not a scientific validation. It specifies the default parameters and warns about input scaling relative to the original PCEN parameter convention. Frozen v2 uses floating-point decoded audio and its own mel-power pipeline; reproductions must preserve that scale. [Versioned documentation](https://librosa.org/doc/0.11.0/generated/librosa.pcen.html).

## Peak confidence, fusion and abstention

**Vio, R., Andreani, P. (2016)**, *The correct estimate of the probability of false detection of the matched filter in weak-signal detection problems*, Astronomy & Astrophysics 589, A20, and **Vio, R., Vergès, C., Andreani, P. (2017)**, part II, A&A 604, A115, are journal articles. They distinguish false-detection probabilities at searched peaks from pointwise Gaussian tails. Their specific noise models are not automatically valid for music-feature correlations; the transferable warning is the selection effect from searching unknown locations. [2016 paper](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2016/05/aa27463-15.pdf), [2017 paper](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2017/08/aa29330-16.pdf).

**Kittler, J., Hatef, M., Duin, R. P. W., Matas, J. (1998)**, *On Combining Classifiers*, IEEE TPAMI 20(3), 226–239, is a journal article on classifier combination. It is background for fusion and its assumptions, not proof that v2's three named families are independent. Distinct method names are not distinct random evidence. [Institutional record](https://dspace.cvut.cz/entities/publication/829206e1-4e28-4091-b0a7-14f2455efaff), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.667881).

**Chow, C. K. (1970)**, *On Optimum Recognition Error and Reject Tradeoff*, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 16(1), 41–46, is a journal article establishing the reject-option trade-off. **El-Yaniv, R., Wiener, Y. (2010)**, *On the Foundations of Noise-free Selective Classification*, JMLR 11, 1605–1641, is a journal article formalizing coverage and selective risk. These justify evaluating refusal and accepted-case error together. Their optimality results or noise-free assumptions cannot be imported into a hand-tuned audio gate. [Chow DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1970.1054406), [JMLR paper](https://jmlr.org/papers/v11/el-yaniv10a.html).

**Geifman, Y., El-Yaniv, R. (2017)**, *Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks*, NeurIPS, is a conference paper. It supports risk-versus-coverage evaluation, while its learned setting differs from this fixed rule engine. [Proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html).

**Guo, C., Pleiss, G., Sun, Y., Weinberger, K. Q. (2017)**, *On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks*, ICML/PMLR 70, 1321–1330, is a conference paper. Calibration concerns correspondence between reported probabilities and empirical correctness. V2 emits no probability; treating its Z statistic as a probability and reporting an ECE would misstate the implemented system. A separately trained probability map would need untouched validation data. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html).

**Angelopoulos, A. N., Bates, S., Candès, E. J., Jordan, M. I., Lei, L.**, *Learn then Test: Calibrating Predictive Algorithms to Achieve Risk Control*, is used here as a 2021 preprint/2022 Stanford working paper, not assigned an unverified peer-reviewed venue. It offers a framework for testing policies on calibration data rather than treating tuned scores as guarantees. [Institutional working-paper page](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/working-papers/learn-then-test-calibrating-predictive-algorithms-achieve-risk).

**Angelopoulos, A. N., Bates, S., Fisch, A., Lei, L., Schuster, T. (2024)**, *Conformal Risk Control*, ICLR, is a conference paper. Expected-loss control under its assumptions is not automatically conditional wrong-accept risk control. Full v2 acceptance can be nonmonotone as floors change because multiple passing clusters cause abstention. Candidate truncation, no-match hypotheses, source dependence, and tuning exposure all require explicit treatment before applying a guarantee. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/f3549ef9b5ff520a7e41ff3cc306ab2b-Abstract-Conference.html).

## What remains defensible

The ingredients—robust spectral representations, correlation, candidate verification, competing peaks, feature fusion, and refusal—are established. The present implementation supports an engineering contribution and a useful internal falsification study. A publishable empirical contribution would require an independently labeled real-audio benchmark, a clear definition of whole-recording versus local-content correctness, serious fingerprint/GCC comparisons, and a reproducible result that survives source-level validation. Neither the literature nor the current small corpus establishes a novel general decision method.

This review does not equate failure to find an identical implementation with novelty. It also does not claim that one historical paper settles every possible future empirical thesis. The main study makes the narrower, evidence-dependent verdict.
