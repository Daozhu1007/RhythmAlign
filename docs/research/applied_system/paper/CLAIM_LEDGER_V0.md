## Core claim boundaries

On fresh, independently timed acoustic recordings from the stress-test domain, frozen RhythmAlign v1.2.0 reduced observed catastrophic wrong placements relative to the specified always-output correlation baselines while retaining 20/24 = 83.3% correct-placement yield. This statement is limited to ten source identities, 24 dependent positive takes, 24 constructed wrong-reference pairs, one domain, and one frozen operating point, with no final-data threshold tuning. It does not include creator recovery cost.
