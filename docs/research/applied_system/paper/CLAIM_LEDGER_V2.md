## Core claim boundaries (V1)

On this fixed acoustic benchmark of controlled rerecordings, frozen RhythmAlign v1.2.0 produced 20 correct placements and four abstentions on 24 dependent positive takes from 10 source identities, with no observed wrong positive accepts, and refused all 24 constructed wrong-reference pairs. The evaluated always-output controls produced more wrong placements (corrected GCC-PHAT 14/24 wrong positives and 24/24 wrong-reference accepts; NCC 5/24 wrong positives and 24/24 wrong-reference accepts), although their estimator and candidate-policy contracts differ from RhythmAlign's, and the comparison does not isolate the causal effect of abstention. All magnitudes concern one domain, one frozen operating point, and a structured 10-identity stress-test pool, with no final-data threshold tuning. Creator recovery cost is unmeasured. No matched selective correlation comparator was evaluated.
