# Owner Pilot Instructions — Fresh Recording Session

Status: WAIT — do not start until the agent says "kit ready" and gives you
the three playback files. This page is the complete script for your ~1
hour of recording. No math, no analysis, no software: play, record, save.

---

## Before you start (one time, ~10 minutes)

1. Find **three recording devices**:
   - **D1** = your main phone (the one you used for the earlier test).
   - **D2** = a second phone, a tablet, or a laptop's built-in microphone.
   - **D3** = one more device: tablet, camera, or second laptop.
   Only two devices? That is acceptable — skip D3's takes (9–12) and tell
   the agent.
2. Find a room where you can play music out loud.
3. Get the **three playback files** from the agent (named `BUF-A.wav`,
   `BUF-B1.wav`, `BUF-BMID.wav`). Put `BUF-A.wav` on the phone whose
   **speaker** you will use (you'll need it for one single take).
4. Everything below plays audio from your **PC speakers**, except take 11.

## The one rule that matters

**Each take = start recording → play the whole file once → stop
recording → save as the take number.**
Leave 2–3 seconds of quiet before you start the playback and after it
ends. Never pause, trim, edit, or convert a recording. If anything goes
wrong, just record the take again and add a letter: `take05_b`.

## Saving

Save every recording into the folder the agent points you to
(`pilot_pack/incoming`), named `take01`, `take02`, … `take12`.
Any file type your device produces is fine (m4a, mp4, wav, …).

---

## The 12 takes (≈2.5 minutes each)

**Normal take, how it works every time:** put the recording device 1–2
meters from the PC speakers, start recording, double-click the buffer
file, let it play to the end, wait 3 seconds, stop recording, save.

| # | Device | Play this | What to do differently |
|---|--------|-----------|------------------------|
| 1 | D1 | BUF-A | nothing — a normal take |
| 2 | D1 | BUF-A | nothing — same as take 1, right after it |
| 3 | D1 | BUF-BMID | nothing — normal take, different file |
| 4 | D1 | BUF-A | turn the volume **down** — quiet but hearable music |
| 5 | D1 | BUF-BMID | gently **tap the desk** near the mic a few times while the music plays |
| 6 | D2 | BUF-A | normal take with the second device |
| 7 | D2 | BUF-A | same again — right after take 6 |
| 8 | D2 | BUF-A | play **another song from a second device** (any different song) softly in the room while the file plays |
| 9 | D3 | BUF-A | normal take with the third device |
| 10 | D3 | BUF-A | volume **down**, like take 4 |
| 11 | D3 | BUF-A | play it from the **phone's own speaker** (the phone with BUF-A on it) instead of the PC speakers; record on D3 as usual |
| 12 | D3 | BUF-B1 | normal take, different file |

Takes 1+2 and 6+7 should feel like twins: same setup, back to back.

## When you're done

Tell the agent: "recordings done" plus anything unusual (a take where a
notification sounded, a volume slip, a retake you kept). That's it — the
agent does all checking, naming, analysis, and reporting.
