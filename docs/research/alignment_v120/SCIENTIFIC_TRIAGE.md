# Scientific Triage — RhythmAlign v1.2.0

## 1. Verdict

**STOP_ENGINEERING_ONLY**

**Do not collect a new confirmatory dataset for the present paper thesis.** The released safeguard addresses a real, causally investigated failure, but verification of matching evidence over time is established audio prior art. The focused search found substantially closer precedents than the previously mapped fingerprint-plus-GCC architecture: explicit rejection of short matched segments, minimum match duration, and checks on the proportion of time containing matches. Recasting these ideas as selective alignment also does not establish a contribution.

This judgment concerns the audio temporal alignment system at **v1.2.0, commit `3a622fc33af1212178296ad9dad57ce9693eed48`**. It is a new scientific assessment, not inheritance of Astra's pre-D1 verdict. D1 materially changed the evaluated algorithm and resolved its documented concentration blocker. Neither successful regression repair nor that earlier blocker determines publication potential.

The remaining empirical possibility is that pooling weak, distributed correlation evidence offers a useful risk-versus-coverage advantage over established fingerprint verification in difficult acoustic recordings. That is conceivable, but current evidence does not establish a comparative anomaly: no fingerprint baseline was fairly reproduced. An unmeasured comparator is insufficient reason to fund new confirmatory acquisition when the proposed mechanism and remedy already have close precedents. This is a conservative research investment decision, not a claim that fingerprints have experimentally defeated v1.2.0.

## 2. Strongest surviving scientific claim

**NONE sufficient to justify the requested confirmatory study.** A sound exploratory finding survives: in the examined pre-D1 system, a brief shared event could dominate signed feature correlation and corroborating representations, leading to an unsupported whole-song placement; D1 converts that attribution into a rejection heuristic. This is valuable mechanism identification and engineering evidence. Its broader lesson—verify the extent and meaning of a content match before extrapolating it to a recording—is already substantially contained in synchronization and fingerprint verification. No independently supported, sufficiently distinctive comparative hypothesis currently lifts it into a paper-oriented study.

Original papers and author-maintained implementation sources were inspected. This is a decision search, not an exhaustive survey or proof that every empirical question has been answered.

The decisive advance over the archived search is therefore not another generic fingerprint citation. It is **explicit segment-duration rejection in audio alignment**, a published **duration/percentage verification rule**, and executable **matched-second occupancy filtering**. Together they remove the main uncertainty about whether the broader support principle exists in comparable audio systems. Their existence is sufficient for this conceptual decision; their exact performance on RhythmAlign recordings remains unknown.

**Would fingerprints naturally reject Astra's failure class?** A pure hash-vote peak need not: many hashes can originate within the same brief event, and agreeing timestamps do not themselves establish temporal extent. A verifier that rejects matched spans shorter than five seconds would reject evidence confined to a one-second span. That is a conditional consequence of its rule, not a measured prediction for the archived pair: actual features, window support and accidental additional hits could enlarge the span. Panako's occupancy check adds protection against sparse matches across a longer span, but its matched-span denominator does not certify support across the whole recording. [Pinned matching implementation](https://github.com/JorenSix/Panako/blob/e4b0e1dbb55e340bc66c90bac0ceb82b2cf84211/src/main/java/be/panako/strategy/panako/PanakoStrategy.java#L400).

Consequently, neither “fingerprints automatically solve it” nor “fingerprints only count votes and miss temporal support” is defensible. The strongest threat is a real verifier with its ordinary duration/occupancy machinery enabled. Finding a vulnerable permissive fingerprint configuration would not defeat that threat. Local-copy retrieval must also retain its native interpretation: a returned shared interval is not automatically an assertion that two whole songs are interchangeable.

## 4. Candidate framing ranking

1. **Empirical failure analysis — strongest remaining possibility, below GO threshold.** Astra provides a natural-audio existence result and causal attribution, rather than only a manufactured adversary. The potentially useful extension is quantitative evidence that correlated feature views repeatedly amplify localized coincidences across independent sources, with a materially different trade-off from established verification. The search did not locate an identical PCEN/HPSS/onset attribution study. Nevertheless, uniqueness of that implementation-level measurement is insufficient: the failure concern and relevant remedy are known, and no cross-system anomaly is yet demonstrated.
2. **Selective temporal alignment — useful evaluation framework, weak contribution.** It forces wrong accepts and useful coverage to be reported together. A contribution would require more than renaming confidence thresholds: for example, a demonstrably better selection policy at matched utility, or valid risk control under explicitly justified sampling and calibration assumptions. The released engine supplies neither probability calibration nor a risk guarantee. Existing selective-classification work already provides the framing. [Geifman & El-Yaniv, NeurIPS 2017](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html).
3. **Temporal-support verification — useful safeguard, defeated methodological thesis.** Prior work explicitly verifies duration and match distribution. D1 is an inexpensive representation-specific realization. The statement that whole-recording placement always requires distributed evidence is also too strong: a uniquely identified common synchronization event can establish one offset if common origin and absence of edits/drift are independently known. The actual issue is unjustified extrapolation when those assumptions are absent.
4. **Target identity versus content occurrence — necessary label distinction, not a released method contribution.** A recording can contain a correctly aligned background source that is not the user's intended target. This clarifies the evaluation contract but does not turn v1.2.0 into an identity-aware system. It also overlaps the older identification/copy-localization distinction. Changing the thesis to solve target intent would change the scientific subject.

## 5. What is genuinely known today

The consolidated `ASTRA_HANDOFF.md`, `FACT_SHEET.md` and `DATA_PROVENANCE.md` are the authoritative local provenance sources for the following facts. Historical counts were not recomputed in this triage.

- **Formal exploratory discovery:** Astra evaluated the pre-v1.2.0 engine, identified an accepted wrong-song pair, attributed its global evidence to a brief region, and used targeted interventions and feature ablations to investigate the mechanism. Its `ENGINEERING_ONLY` / `RELEASE_BLOCKER_FOUND` conclusions remain valid historical judgments about that engine. The study is the hypothesis-generation foundation for D1, not discarded pre-release evaluation.
- **Concrete mechanism:** `tr_lingduihua` → `tr_yanwulieche` accepted around **+1.462857 s**, with about **148.57 s geometric overlap**, while roughly **94.563%** of net signed plain-PCEN correlation came from approximately one second. Corroboration across feature representations did not supply independent evidence about temporal extent.
- **Design response:** D1 verifies the deciding PCEN member, bins its signed contribution in nominal one-second reference-time bins, and downgrades an otherwise accepted candidate when maximum-bin share exceeds **0.25**. It shipped unchanged in the frozen release.
- **Development calibration:** 18 component-A wrong accepts and 46 previously accepted positives informed design/threshold selection. The latter include 29 real pairs without independent precise timing labels apart from one manual interval. Calling them preservation targets is justified; calling every offset independently verified is not.
- **Spent validation:** the 156 component-B wrong-song pairs were a holdout when D1 was designed. That historical separation is meaningful and should be reported honestly. Their subsequent evaluation and reruns mean they are unavailable as a fresh confirmatory set. Rejection of known counterexamples and preservation of known positives establish regression behavior, not a population guarantee.
- **Reusable scientific infrastructure:** the archived `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md` already defines offset tolerances, exact/manual/unknown/no-match ground-truth strata, selective-risk reporting, and restrictions on independence claims. The contribution-attribution, intervention, mismatch-search and regression tooling in `experiments/alignment_research/`, plus the alignment evaluation and D1 packages, remain usable where compatible with the release. Their outputs stay exploratory/development evidence. There is no reason to rediscover these protocols or reimplement the discovery machinery.

There is **no untouched real-audio confirmatory population**. Existing GCC-PHAT success on construction-linked positives does not establish an acoustic-recording advantage, and its forced argmax is not a fair substitute for a calibrated rejecting baseline. The absent fingerprint comparison is an acknowledged limitation, not evidence in RhythmAlign's favor.

## 6. What remains unproven

**The released statistic's meaning.** At a fixed candidate offset, let signed bin contributions be `S_i`, total `S = sum(S_i) > 0`, and `c = max(S_i)/S`. D1 accepts this gate when `c <= 0.25`. This has an attribution interpretation, but no demonstrated likelihood-ratio, calibrated-probability or population-risk interpretation. Negative contributions make the normalized bins a signed measure; cancellation can produce `c > 1`. Thus 0.25 is not “25% of independently verified matching content.” These properties follow from the targeted `_concentration_profile` and `_apply_temporal_support` sections of the released engine.

Even with nonnegative contributions, four equally contributing adjacent bins satisfy the threshold while the rest of a long overlap contributes nothing. That algebraic example concerns the gate alone, not a claimed runnable whole-engine counterexample. It shows why the rule does not certify whole-recording support, separated regions or target identity. Bin placement, feature dynamics, overlap duration and signed cancellation can affect the score. The retained geometric-overlap floor measures available overlap, not verified content duration.

The exact **one-second / 0.25** choice is a convenient development-selected heuristic. The broader support concept is scientifically meaningful but established. Therefore the answer to the contribution choice is **C: neither the exact statistic nor the broader concept is presently a defensible new contribution**. This does not make the safeguard unreasonable engineering.

Entropy, effective support length and Gini-style concentration summaries would require a justified treatment of signed values before their usual distributional interpretations apply. Positive-only normalization would change the evidence being summarized. Counting separated supporting regions more directly tests spread, but requires defining a reliable local match; the D1 development work already found difficulties with local-window verification on its low-SNR positives. Those findings should be reused, not erased because alternatives sound more principled. They do not establish that fingerprint verification has the same difficulty.

For fixed features and offset, deleting the strongest bin leaves `S * (1 - c)`: concentration already measures that particular leave-bin-out loss. It is **not** equivalent to re-extracting features or rerunning peak selection, competing offsets, standardized scores and the complete decision after removing a region. The D1 design note's stated relationship to leave-region-out verification should be interpreted at this limited attribution level. No replacement is proposed or implemented here.

If a future comparative claim were independently motivated, it would need to distinguish concentration from ordinary duration/occupancy verification and from one defensible alternative support summary; simply trying many summaries and publishing the best TEST result would not establish the released rule. At present, listing alternatives mainly exposes how underdetermined the specific statistic is.

**Generalization and utility.** Unknowns include false-accept prevalence, transport of 0.25 to unseen identities and acoustic paths, coverage loss when genuine evidence is intermittently audible, sensitivity to shared samples and repeated sections, and comparative risk at useful coverage. Different edits/remasters can share valid intervals without admitting one globally correct offset. These cases require explicit labels; they cannot be counted automatically as either ordinary positives or wrong-song negatives. None of these uncertainties is resolved by inherited zero-error rows.

**The residual room-grid case.** `haiditan_ds_1` × `tr_hongzhoutian` remains accepted around **+59.72 s**, with deciding-member top-bin share about **0.061**. Its true label is unresolved. It cannot be used as a proven wrong accept, a proven correct placement, or confirmation of distributed-support generalization.

Resolving it requires establishing which exact recordings are audible, when each occurs, which recording the user intended to place, and whether that target has a unique constant offset. Independent synchronized playback/source records, if they exist, plus adjudicated timing anchors across the overlap would be stronger than listening at the proposed offset alone. Unrecoverable source identity must remain unknown.

Distributed background music could correctly support an acoustic offset while supporting the wrong target for the user. The gate cannot distinguish those situations. More temporal support addresses localized-evidence failures; it does not identify intent or prove recording equivalence. The older fingerprint literature explicitly recognizes identification of multiple sources in a mixture, so this semantic boundary is not itself a new discovery. [Wang, §3.1](https://www.ee.columbia.edu/~dpwe/papers/Wang03-shazam.pdf).

## 7. Is new data collection scientifically justified?

**NO, for the present v1.2.0 paper proposition.**

The evidence supports a useful engineering account: exploratory falsification exposed a failure of the original acceptance logic; an attribution-based safeguard repaired it; known regressions were preserved. New data could establish transportability of that repair. Transportability testing alone, however, would primarily validate a deployment decision using an established verification principle.

An empirical paper need not introduce a new algorithm. It could justify acquisition by exposing an important limitation in established systems, demonstrating a new regime where usual verification sacrifices useful coverage, or supplying a broadly reusable task distinction with an independently measured consequence. Astra currently establishes none of these comparative gaps. It establishes a failure of one previous system and a successful development response. The fine-grained attribution is informative, but the generalization “strong local matches are insufficient to establish longer content identity” is already anticipated by the nearest work.

The strongest counterargument is that global pooling may retain genuine low-SNR alignments that local fingerprint verification rejects. D1's development investigation makes that technically plausible. It is still a proposed performance advantage over an unmeasured baseline, with little independent real timing ground truth. I do not treat plausibility as enough to commission a paper-oriented dataset in this triage.

This is **not UNRESOLVED_PRIOR_ART**: accessible primary evidence resolves the central question about temporal extent and rejection. The remaining uncertainty is empirical competitiveness, not whether the verification concept is known. It is **not PROCEED_METHOD_STUDY** because no methodological novelty hypothesis survives. It falls below **PROCEED_EMPIRICAL_STUDY** because the current empirical gap is too implementation-specific and insufficiently established to justify the requested investment. No publication venue is recommended.

## 9. Kill criteria

The first three stop conditions are already satisfied at the conceptual level. The remaining rows specify what would defeat attempts to revive the same claims; they are falsification boundaries, not instructions to run experiments.

A fingerprint match with accurate local boundaries must not be scored as a false whole-song assertion unless the evaluated baseline actually makes that assertion under the frozen task contract. Conversely, a forced GCC-PHAT argmax must not be the sole comparator for rejection. Any renewed competitiveness argument would require an actual fingerprint verifier, calibrated GCC-PHAT rejection, the released engine, its support-gate ablation, and a simple correlation-confidence selector; DTW would be relevant only if nonconstant timing became part of a separately defined question. These are fairness conditions, not an approved experiment slate.
